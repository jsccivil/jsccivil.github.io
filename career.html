Directorship — 2016 to 2022

Six years running a sixty-year-old institution

Dr. Chauhan led Samrat Ashok Technological Institute as its Director from August 2016 to February 2022 — a grant-in-aid autonomous institution of the Government of Madhya Pradesh, founded in 1960. What changed during those six years:

Accreditation & autonomy
Declared an autonomous institution by the UGC in 2015–16; NAAC accreditation in 2016; NBA accreditation for Mechanical and Electronics & Instrumentation in 2018, and for Civil and Computer Science in 2021.
Centres of Excellence
Nine established across the institute, two of them in his own field — cost-effective building materials & construction techniques, and advanced geopolymer concrete — alongside additive manufacturing, embedded design & simulation, a LabVIEW academy, virtual instrumentation & IoT, AI & machine learning, the Ramanujan Design Centre, and a centre for non-violence & peace in education.
National standing
SATI was one of 92 institutions nationwide to secure a TEQIP-III grant (₹15.5 crore), and took first place in India in the programme's 2019 performance audit — earning further allocations of ₹1.5 crore and ₹1.0 crore. The institute was made nodal centre for the Ministry's Smart India Hackathon Grand Finale in 2020.
Partnerships
Twenty-four memoranda of understanding signed between 2015 and 2020, including Louisiana Tech University, Ruston, USA (2020), the National Highway Authority of India, CSIR-AMPRI, VNIT Nagpur, CIDC and the Indian Society for Trenchless Technology.
Convening
Host of the 44th Indian Social Science Congress in March 2021, and of an international seminar with HUDCO on Formal Solutions to Informal Settlements in 2019, drawing delegates from 22 countries. Around 200 webinars, expert talks and short-term training programmes were run through the pandemic.
Administration
Examination reform brought semester results down to 48 hours; a graduation ceremony was instituted; twelve collaborative research projects were placed in progress.
Case — est. 1991

CETDEC — a building centre that builds arguments in brick

The Civil Engineering Technology Development Centre, founded on the SATI campus in 1991 as a HUDCO-approved institutional building centre, demonstrates low-cost construction by building it: experimental housing, school buildings, sericulture sheds and public works erected with alternative materials and techniques. Under Dr. Chauhan's stewardship it earned a KfW (German) grant, a national HUDCO Silver Plaque (1995), and commissions worth ₹1.5 crore from the district administration — and its methods were documented in a twelve-volume pocket-book series planned for worldwide circulation through UNCHS (UN-Habitat).
