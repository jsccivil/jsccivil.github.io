Case — est. 1991

CETDEC — a building centre that builds arguments in brick

The Civil Engineering Technology Development Centre, founded on the SATI campus in 1991 as a HUDCO-approved institutional building centre, demonstrates low-cost construction by building it: experimental housing, school buildings, sericulture sheds and public works erected with alternative materials and techniques. Under Dr. Chauhan's stewardship it earned a KfW (German) grant, a national HUDCO Silver Plaque (1995), and commissions worth ₹1.5 crore from the district administration — and its methods were documented in a twelve-volume pocket-book series planned for worldwide circulation through UNCHS (UN-Habitat).
